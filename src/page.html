02  BEFORE YOU GO出发之前

Before you go出发之前

Fares, passes, weather and the tipping arithmetic — including the parts our own sources got wrong.票价、通票、天气和小费算法——包括我们自己的来源讲错的部分。

Getting in from the airport从机场进城

  • Blue Line runs 24/7 from inside O'Hare to the Loop. 40-50 minutes, $5.00 flat. Rideshare is roughly $35-65+ and no faster in traffic.蓝线 24 小时从奥黑尔机场直达市中心，40-50 分钟，统一 $5.00。打车大约 $35-65 以上，堵起来并不更快。
  • From Midway, the Orange Line reaches the Loop in 25-30 minutes at the standard rail fare. It does not run 24 hours.中途机场坐橙线到市中心 25-30 分钟，普通票价。橙线不是 24 小时运营。

Paying for transit交通怎么付钱

  • You do not need a Ventra card. Tap a contactless credit card or Apple/Google Pay straight onto the reader at any turnstile or bus farebox.不需要办 Ventra 卡。直接用带感应功能的信用卡或 Apple/Google Pay 在闸机、公交刷卡机上一拍就行。
  • Bus $2.25. Rail $2.50. Blue Line originating at O'Hare $5.00.公交 $2.25，地铁 $2.50，从奥黑尔上车的蓝线 $5.00。
  • 1-, 3-, 7- and 30-day unlimited passes exist. We could not verify current 2026 prices, so check transitchicago.com/fares rather than trusting a number here.有 1 天、3 天、7 天、30 天的不限次通票。我们没能核实 2026 年的现价，请自己查 transitchicago.com/fares，不要信这里给的数字。

Attraction passes景点通票

  • CityPASS is $144 adult / $114 child, valid 9 days. Shedd and Skydeck are compulsory, then you pick three more. It works only if you genuinely want five paid attractions.CityPASS 成人 $144、儿童 $114，9 天有效。谢德水族馆和西尔斯塔是固定的，再从名单里选三个。只有你真的想去五个收费景点时才划算。
  • Go City Explorer from $109 lets you choose how many attractions, 30 days to use them. The more flexible of the two.Go City Explorer $109 起，自己选去几个景点，30 天内用完，是两者中更灵活的。
  • Go City All-Inclusive from $149 is the one to be sceptical of. It is priced assuming four to six attractions a day, which almost nobody actually does while also eating, walking and resting.Go City All-Inclusive $149 起，这个要多留个心眼。它的定价假设你一天刷四到六个景点，而实际上你还要吃饭、走路、休息，几乎没人做得到。

When to come什么时候来

  • Late May to early October is the reliable window. June, July and September are the sweet spot: warm, long daylight, and every river cruise and outdoor attraction running.5 月底到 10 月初是稳妥的窗口。6 月、7 月、9 月最舒服：暖和、白天长，游船和户外项目全部正常运营。
  • Winter is a real constraint, not a minor inconvenience. December to February sits in the low-to-mid 30s°F with lows in the teens, and the wind off the lake makes it worse.冬天是实打实的限制，不是小小的不方便。12 月到 2 月最高温只有 0°C 上下，最低到零下十度左右，湖上的风还会让体感更冷。
  • Book the architecture river cruise one to two weeks ahead for a peak-season weekend. A few days is usually enough midweek.旺季周末的建筑游船要提前一到两周订。平日提前几天一般就够。
  • Museum free days are frequently restricted to Illinois residents with ID. Read the fine print before you build a day around one.博物馆的免费日经常只限伊利诺伊州居民并要查证件。别为了免费日安排一整天，先看清楚条款。

Money花钱

  • Tip 18-20% at sit-down restaurants. 15% now reads as a below-average tip.正式餐厅小费 18-20%。给 15% 现在会被理解成"不太满意"。
  • Chicago's combined sales tax is 10.5%, among the highest of any major US city, and downtown restaurant bills run higher still.芝加哥的综合销售税是 10.5%，在美国大城市里数一数二，市中心餐厅的账单还要更高。
  • Many restaurants now add an automatic 3-5% service or kitchen fee on top of tax and separate from your tip. Check the bill before you tip again.很多餐厅现在会自动加 3-5% 的服务费或后厨费，是在税之外、也和小费分开的。再给小费之前先看一眼账单。

On safety关于安全

  • Chicago is a normal major US city for visitors. The Loop, River North, Streeterville, Lincoln Park, Lakeview, Wicker Park, West Loop and Hyde Park are all standard tourist ground.对游客来说芝加哥就是一座普通的美国大城市。卢普区、河北区、Streeterville、林肯公园、Lakeview、Wicker Park、西环、海德公园都是常规游客区。
  • "Avoid the South Side" is bad geography, and this guide's own Xiaohongshu source repeated it. Hyde Park is on the South Side, and it holds the University of Chicago and the Griffin Museum of Science and Industry. It is a normal place to spend a day."别往南边走"这个说法在地理上就是错的，而这份攻略的小红书来源恰好也这么讲。海德公园就在南边，芝加哥大学和科学与工业博物馆都在那儿，去待一天完全正常。
  • The precaution that is actually well documented is car break-ins. Lock a rental car and leave nothing visible inside.真正有据可查的风险是车辆被砸。租来的车一定锁好，车里不要留任何看得见的东西。
  • Use ordinary late-night judgement on the CTA, particularly the Red Line in the small hours. That is a reason to pay attention, not a reason to avoid the trains.深夜坐 CTA 保持基本警觉，尤其是凌晨的红线。这是让你留意，不是让你不坐地铁。

09  WHAT TO SKIP可以跳过的

What to skip可以跳过的

Popular things that do not repay the time.很红但不值得花时间的。

Navy Pier
4.0 across 15,000+ reviews is respectable, not exceptional, and recent visitors say there is little to do beyond walking and eating chain food — the Ferris wheel photo takes an hour, not the half-day every guide budgets.一万五千多条评价平均4.0分，只能算及格而非出色，近期评价普遍说除了散步和吃连锁餐饮没什么可做；摩天轮那张照片一小时就够，不值得像各种攻略说的排半天。
360 CHICAGO (John Hancock Center observation deck) + TILT
You are paying an add-on on top of general admission for a ride multiple reviewers clock at about 90 seconds against an advertised three minutes; if you want one deck, Willis Tower Skydeck is the better single purchase.在普通门票之外还要另加钱，而多位游客实测这个倾斜体验只有约90秒，官方却宣传三分钟；如果只上一个观景台，威利斯大厦更划算。
Museum of Ice Cream Chicago
$24-30 per adult for candy-colored photo rooms, and the review thread splits hard between delight and 'overrated for the price' — it is a photo set, not a museum.成人票24至30美元，换来的是几间糖果色拍照房，评价明显两极，'价格虚高'的抱怨反复出现——它是布景，不是博物馆。
Tetto
Open May through October only, so it fails as a year-round recommendation, and 3.7 on Google across 111 reviews with repeated service complaints is below the bar for a hyped rooftop.只在5月到10月营业，根本谈不上全年推荐；谷歌111条评价3.7分，服务问题被反复提及，对一个被吹得很火的屋顶酒吧来说不合格。
Indiana Dunes National Park (skyline mirage from the beach)
The floating-skyline mirage needs a temperature inversion that happens a handful of days a year, and you pay $25 per vehicle to drive 30-50 miles for a phenomenon no trip can guarantee.天际线悬浮的海市蜃楼需要特定的逆温条件，一年只出现有限几天；却要开车三十到五十英里、每车缴25美元门票，去赌一个无法保证的景象。
Frato's Culinary Kitchen (Frato's Pizza)
Schaumburg is a 45-60 minute drive each way for a 4.1-rated suburban restaurant, and the 13-inch mozzarella stick that drives the hype is served Sundays only.Schaumburg 单程要开45到60分钟，只为一家评分4.1的郊区餐厅，而带火它的那根13英寸马苏里拉芝士条还只在周日供应。

10  WHERE THE TWO FEEDS DISAGREE两个平台的分歧在哪

Where the two feeds disagree两个平台的分歧在哪

The differences are structural, not random. They follow from what each platform rewards.这些差异是结构性的，不是随机的，源自两个平台各自奖励什么。

The unit of recommendation differs推荐的单位不一样

TikTok recommends a dish. Xiaohongshu recommends a visit. A Chicago TikTok hit is forty-five seconds on one item, and viewers arrive ordering that and nothing else. An XHS note is structured as a whole outing: 人均, the room, the photo angle, parking.TikTok 推荐的是一道菜，小红书推荐的是一次出行。芝加哥的 TikTok 爆款就是 45 秒讲一样东西，看完的人到店只点那一样。小红书笔记则是一整趟安排：人均、环境、出片点、停车。

They optimise for different frames两边优化的是不同的画面

TikTok needs motion and sound — the cheese pull, the crunch, the pour. Xiaohongshu needs a still frame — 出片 lighting, a wood-toned room, a window seat with skyline. That pushes the two feeds toward genuinely different venues.TikTok 要的是动态和声音：拉丝、脆响、浇汁。小红书要的是一张静态照片：出片的光线、木色调的空间、能拍到天际线的窗边位。这让两边推的店从根上就不一样。

The cuisine skew is real菜系上的偏差是真实存在的

Xiaohongshu's Chicago is heavily Chinese and East Asian — Chinatown, hotpot, Korean Lakeview, boba, omakase as the splurge. TikTok's Chicago is American-viral — Italian beef, deep dish, tavern thin crust, birria, smash burgers. Use one feed only and you get a genuinely incomplete city.小红书的芝加哥重心在中餐和东亚菜：中国城、火锅、Lakeview 的韩餐、奶茶，贵一点就是日料 omakase。TikTok 的芝加哥是美式爆款：意式牛肉、深盘、酒吧薄脆、birria、smash burger。只看一边，你拿到的是一座不完整的城市。

They go stale in opposite directions两边过期的方式正好相反

TikTok churns fast, so the risk is arriving after the viral moment collapsed — or at a second location that is nothing like the original. Xiaohongshu ranks on accumulated saves, so a three-year-old note still sits at the top for a restaurant that has changed chef, price or owner. Ask TikTok is this still hot, and Xiaohongshu is this still true.TikTok 更新极快，风险是你到的时候热度已经过去了，或者去的是和本店完全不一样的二店。小红书按收藏量排序，所以三年前的笔记还挂在最上面，而那家店可能早就换了厨师、涨了价、易了主。对 TikTok 要问还火吗，对小红书要问还准吗。

Only one of them tells you the price and the queue只有一边会告诉你价格和排队

Xiaohongshu states 人均 as a matter of convention, and routinely gives 排队 tactics by time of day. TikTok almost never states a price — a $28 sandwich and a $9 sandwich look identical on camera — and shows the line as social proof rather than telling you how to avoid it.小红书写人均几乎是惯例，还会告诉你几点去排队最短。TikTok 基本不提价格——$28 的三明治和 $9 的在镜头里长得一样——而且拍队伍是为了证明它火，不是为了教你怎么避开。

How this was made这份攻略是怎么做的

Seven parallel research sweeps covered TikTok from different angles; a separate pass covered Xiaohongshu. Every candidate was then handed to a verification step that checked it against live sources — open or closed, current rating, address, closed days — with instructions to drop rather than keep when the evidence did not hold. 7 places were dropped that way, including one permanently closed and one closing in September 2026.七路并行的检索从不同角度覆盖 TikTok，另有一路覆盖小红书。所有候选地点都进了核实环节，对照实时来源逐条检查——是否还开着、当前评分、地址、休息日——并且明确要求证据不足时删掉而不是保留。这样删掉了 7 个地点，其中一个已永久关闭，一个将在 2026 年 9 月关闭。

Both language versions are written, not machine-translated. Where a fact could not be verified, the page says so rather than filling the gap — CTA multi-day pass prices are the clearest example.两种语言都是分别写的，不是机器翻译。凡是核不实的信息，页面会直接说明，而不是拿东西填上——CTA 多日通票的价格就是最明显的例子。
